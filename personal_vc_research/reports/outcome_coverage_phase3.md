## 1. Republic holdings disclosures — what they are and are not

* **Sources.** Four filings by Republic's commission-securities vehicle (filer CIK 1794805):
  * Form C (Republic Note), filed 2023-02-07, as of 2022-12-31: 422 rows.
  * Three Form C-AR (Republic Core):
    * filed 2024-04-30, as of 2023-12-31: 508 rows
    * filed 2025-04-30, as of 2024-12-31: 609 rows
    * filed 2026-04-30, as of 2025-12-31: 645 rows
  * Parsed rows: `data/tables/republic_holdings_rows.csv`, with accession, page, raw line and sha256. Verbatim
    legends: `republic_holdings_legends.csv`.
* **Scope (filer's own words).** The table lists "the securities Republic Crowd-Invest has acquired as partial
  compensation" (its ~2% commission), "in the same form and manner as the securities sold". It is "prepared
  internally" and "unaudited".
  * Each row is **Republic's own holding** for one issuance. It is not an issuer record and not retail-investor
    data.
  * An SEC-hosted filing is still the filer's claim, not SEC verification.
* **Marks (verbatim, identical across years apart from line breaks):**
  * `*` "To Republic Crowd-Invest's knowledge the issuer is insolvent, dormant, or has dissolved and Republic
    Crowd-Invest does not expect to receive any cash proceeds as a result of its securities holdings."
    * This is a **composite** claim; no component is selected.
  * `+` "Security has converted due to a qualifying equity financing, liquidity event or equivalent event for a
    digital asset security, but Republic Crowd-Invest has not realized cash proceeds from such conversion."
  * `&` (2023+) The commission column is valued at issuance. It is not fair value.
  * Numbered notes:
    * 2022 uses superscripts on the row (e.g. "Ring4⁶") with notes "1."–"10." describing issuer repurchases,
      tender offers or acquisitions at N¢ on the dollar.
    * 2023+ uses "(n)" after the name, with notes reading "The securities were liquidated, resulting in an
      approximate cash payout of N cents on the dollar to be contributed to the Republic Note dividend pool."
    * The numbering is independent between the two layouts.
* **Timing.** The holdings as-of date is **not** the event date. For a `*`, only the **first appearance of the
  mark** can be dated, within (last as-of date listed unmarked, first as-of date marked]. The underlying event
  may precede that window by any amount; an earlier unmarked row does not show the event had not occurred.
* **Inconsistencies observed:**
  * Issuance dates differ across filings for 12 of the offering-level series (e.g. Canopy: 3/16/2022 in 2022,
    2021-07 later).
  * One holding is absent from one year's table (Genius Juice: present 2022, absent 2023, present 2024–2025).
  * Marks appear and disappear (Hemster `+` 2022 only).
  * Under our rules, a missing or disappearing mark establishes nothing.
* **Claims table.** `data/tables/republic_holdings_claims_40.csv` has 157 rows, one per offering × filing row.
  Each row records:
  * accession, page, row text, listed entity and issuance month;
  * as-of date and filing date;
  * the exact mark, its verbatim legend and any verbatim footnote text;
  * entity-match class and evidence, interpretation, uncertainty and series id.
* **Repeated observations.** Repeated yearly rows of one holding share a `series_id` and are repeated
  observations, not new events. 33 of the 40 offerings have more than one yearly row.

## 2. Janover contradiction (time-boxed, well under 1 analyst-hour)

* **The row.** "Janover Ventures*" (2020-12, $714,053) appears on the C-AR filed 2026-04-30, p.57 (as of
  2025-12-31). The same holding is unmarked as of 2022, 2023 and 2024. Rendered crops confirm all four years.
* **The match is solid.** The 2022 table date 12/28/2020 equals the C-U close, and the label and capital are
  identical.
* **The listing.** Janover/DeFi Development Corp. is listed:
  * 8-A12B filed 2023-07-19 and three 424B4s filed 2023-07-26.
  * 10-Ks filed 2024, 2025 and 2026.
* **Classification.** An **unresolved source inconsistency**. Both claims are retained. The `*` is excluded from
  the sensitivity set because it conflicts with issuer filings. The strict label `listed_public` rests on issuer
  filings only.
* **Hypotheses, none tested to resolution:**
  * Republic's own commission SAFE did not participate in the pooled conversion, and Republic expects no cash
    from that position.
  * A clerical mark.
  * The LLC→Inc. conversion.

## 11. Recommendations

### A — Business-outcome modeling

**Do not start detailed historical-feature extraction or modeling now.**

* **Labels.** Strict definitive labels exist for **2 of 36** non-withdrawn offerings: one listing and one
  documented operational closure.
  * 34 are unresolved.
  * Unresolved is mainly driven by (a) issuer reporting going dark after a C-TR and (b) an outcome signal that is
    a **composite platform claim** (`*`).
  * The `*` cannot separate insolvent from dormant from dissolved, and it dates Republic's knowledge, not the
    event.
* **Sample size.** 40 offerings cannot establish predictive performance in any case.
* **Next step (superseded by the phase-4 decision to close the study).** The cheapest step that could change the
  picture was **user registry imports** for the 36 issuers with an issuer-reported close: about 2 minutes each, roughly 1.2 h of the user's time, via `data/user_imports/`.
  * Registry status is the only admissible route to `dissolved_or_liquidated_documented` that is not blocked in
    principle.
  * The import pipeline already applies such records to legal-status fields only.
* **Full-frame expansion: not recommended (superseded in phase 4).** The phase-3 projections of events in the
  224-offering frame, and their intervals, are withdrawn (`docs/statistical_interpretation_review.md`):
  * They were unweighted despite year-stratified sampling.
  * With 0–1 events per stratum, no interval is defensible.
  * They measured ascertainment by this collection route, not outcome frequency.
  * Observed counts only: strict definitive labels 0/10, 1/13 and 1/13 in the 2018, 2019 and 2020 cohorts, among
    offerings with an issuer-reported close. These are not population base rates.

### B — Investor-return modeling

**Not supportable from accessible historical sources.**

* **Cash flows.** No investor cash flows were recovered from accessible sources for any of the 40 offerings.
* **The only payoff-like items:**
  * Republic's numbered notes, which describe **Republic's own commission holding** (one sampled offering, Ring4,
    about 100¢). These are platform claims, not retail recovery.
  * Linen's unquantified "return the remaining capital to investors" announcement.
  * Janover's issuer-wide pooled SAFE conversion (level 1 only; conflicting amounts; no per-investor data and no
    sale proceeds).
* **The path forward** is prospective, documented tracking of positions the user actually observes or holds. It
  is not historical inference, and it is not a reason to invest.
